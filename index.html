What is Climate?

Climate refers to the average weather conditions of a place over a long period of time, usually more than 30 years. It includes temperature, rainfall, humidity and wind patterns.

Monsoon Type Climate of India

India has a monsoon type climate. The word monsoon is derived from the Arabic word “mausim”, meaning season. The most important feature of monsoon is the seasonal reversal of wind direction.

Factors Affecting Climate of India

  • Latitude: Tropic of Cancer divides India into tropical and subtropical regions
  • Altitude: Himalayas block cold winds from Central Asia
  • Pressure and Wind System: Seasonal changes influence monsoon
  • Distance from the Sea: Coastal regions have moderate climate
  • Relief Features: Western Ghats cause heavy rainfall

India’s Climate Diversity

India experiences diverse climatic conditions due to its vast size, relief features and monsoon winds.

Cold Weather Season

December–February. Low temperatures and western disturbances bring light rain.

Hot Weather Season

March–May. High temperatures, loo winds and dust storms.

South-West Monsoon

June–September. Brings about 75% of annual rainfall.

Retreating Monsoon

October–November. Cyclones affect eastern coast.

Importance of Monsoon

  • Main source of water for agriculture
  • Recharges rivers and groundwater
  • Controls cropping patterns
  • Influences Indian economy
