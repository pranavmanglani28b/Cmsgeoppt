What is Climate?

Climate refers to the average weather conditions of a place over a long period of time, usually more than 30 years. It includes temperature, rainfall, humidity and wind.

Monsoon Type Climate of India

India has a monsoon type climate. The seasonal reversal of winds is the most important characteristic of the monsoon. About 75% of the annual rainfall is received during the south-west monsoon season.

Factors Affecting Climate

  • Latitude
  • Altitude
  • Pressure and wind systems
  • Distance from the sea
  • Relief features such as Himalayas
