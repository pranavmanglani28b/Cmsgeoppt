What is Climate?

Climate refers to the average weather conditions of a place over a long period of time, usually more than 30 years.

Monsoon Climate

India has a monsoon type climate due to seasonal reversal of winds. The term monsoon comes from the Arabic word mausim.

Factors Affecting Climate

  • Latitude
  • Altitude
  • Pressure & Winds
  • Distance from Sea
  • Relief (Himalayas)

Seasons of India

Cold Weather, Hot Weather, Advancing Monsoon and Retreating Monsoon are the four seasons.

Importance of Monsoon

Monsoon controls agriculture, water supply, cropping patterns and India’s economy.

Rainfall Distribution

Western Ghats and North-East receive heavy rainfall, while Rajasthan receives very little rainfall.
