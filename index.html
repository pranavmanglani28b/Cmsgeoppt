Climate of India

Class 8 Geography • NCERT Based Explanation of Indian Climate, Seasons and Monsoon System

What is Climate?

Climate refers to the sum total of weather conditions and variations over a large area for a long period of time. Weather may change within a day, but climate changes over many years.

Climate of India

India has a monsoon type of climate. The monsoon is experienced in the tropical area roughly between 20° N and 20° S. The seasonal reversal in wind direction during a year is the characteristic feature of the monsoon.

Factors Affecting India's Climate

  • Latitude: Tropic of Cancer divides India into tropical and subtropical regions.
  • Altitude: Mountains like the Himalayas prevent cold winds from Central Asia.
  • Pressure and Wind System: Seasonal changes affect wind direction.
  • Distance from the Sea: Coastal areas have moderate climate.
  • Relief Features: Mountains influence rainfall distribution.

Cold Weather Season

The cold weather season begins from December to February. The temperature decreases from south to north. Light winter rainfall occurs due to western disturbances.

Hot Weather Season

From March to May, temperatures rise rapidly. Hot dry winds called Loo blow in northern plains. Dust storms are common.

Advancing Monsoon

The south-west monsoon arrives in early June. It brings heavy rainfall and is vital for agriculture. India receives about 75% of its rainfall during this season.

Retreating Monsoon

From October to November, monsoon winds withdraw. Cyclones may develop over the Bay of Bengal causing heavy rain in coastal areas.

Importance of Monsoon

  • Essential for agriculture
  • Refills rivers and reservoirs
  • Controls vegetation and cropping patterns
  • Affects economy and daily life
