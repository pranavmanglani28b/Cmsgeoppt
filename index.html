Monsoon Climate of India

India has a monsoon type climate characterized by seasonal reversal of winds and unequal distribution of rainfall.

Cold Weather Season

December–February • Low temperatures • Clear skies

Hot Weather Season

March–May • Heat waves • Rising temperatures

South-West Monsoon

June–September • Heavy rainfall • Agriculture depends on it

Retreating Monsoon

October–November • Cyclones on eastern coast
